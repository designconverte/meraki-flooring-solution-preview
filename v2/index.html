The process

From the first visit to the last threshold.

  1. Step 01

    Free in-home estimate

    We measure, check the subfloor and walk you through the options. The estimate is free.

    Material and acclimation

Questions

Do you supply the flooring?

Yes. We supply the material and install it, so one team is responsible from choice to finish. If you already bought your flooring, tell us in the request.

How long does a project take?

Most projects start and finish in about a week. Smaller homes can take a day. We confirm the schedule at the estimate.

What does the warranty cover?

Our workmanship, for 1 year after the install. If something we installed shows a problem, we come back and make it right.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance.

Can you refinish the hardwood I already have?

Yes. We sand it to bare wood and apply a new finish. It's labor and finish only; no new flooring to buy.

How much does a new floor cost?

It depends on the floor, the rooms and the subfloor. We give you a written estimate after the free in-home visit.

Which areas do you serve?

Charlotte and 22 nearby communities in North and South Carolina. Not on the list? Send the request with your ZIP and we'll let you know.
