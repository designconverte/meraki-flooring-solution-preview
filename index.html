Why Meraki

A new floor isn't a purchase. It's an investment in your home. So we don't compete on the lowest price. We compete on value: every board, every seam, every threshold.

  1. 01

    We show up prepared.

    Not with a tape in a pocket and a notebook. We bring a folder with our work and the materials, and walk you through the options for your rooms.

  2. 02

    One team, material to finish.

    We supply the flooring and install it, so one team answers for the result, from the first box to the last threshold.

  3. 03

    Backed after we leave.

    Every install carries a 1-year workmanship warranty. If something we installed shows a problem, we come back and make it right. Fully insured: general liability and workers' comp.

Services

Eight floors. One standard.

Not sure which floor fits? That's what the estimate visit is for.

  1. 01 Hardwood Floor Installation Our core work, about 8 in 10 jobs. Wide plank and herringbone, with the subfloor prepped before the first board.
  2. 02 LVP Installation Luxury vinyl plank, laid with the same prep and care as hardwood.
  3. 03 Laminate Flooring Installation Laminate set tight and square, with clean transitions from room to room.
  4. 04 Hardwood Sanding & Refinishing Bring an existing hardwood floor back: sanded to bare wood, then a new finish. Labor only: you keep your floor.
  5. 05 Stair Installation & Refinishing New hardwood stairs built, or treads and handrails refinished to match the floor.
  6. 06 Tile Installation Tile floors set by the same team that handles the rest of the house.
  7. 07 Carpet Installation Carpet for the rooms where you want it soft underfoot.
  8. 08 VCT Installation Vinyl composition tile for laundry, utility and work spaces.

Do you supply the flooring?

Yes. We supply the material and install it, so one team is responsible from choice to finish. If you already bought your flooring, tell us in the request.

How long does a project take?

Most projects start and finish in about a week. Smaller homes can take a day. We confirm the schedule at the estimate.

What does the warranty cover?

Our workmanship, for 1 year after the install. If something we installed shows a problem, we come back and make it right.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance.

Can you refinish the hardwood I already have?

Yes. We sand it to bare wood and apply a new finish. It's labor and finish only, with no new flooring to buy.

How much does a new floor cost?

It depends on the floor, the rooms and the subfloor. We give you a written estimate after the free in-home visit.

Which areas do you serve?

Charlotte and 22 nearby communities in North and South Carolina. Not on the list? Send the request with your ZIP and we'll let you know.
